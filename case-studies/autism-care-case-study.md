# Case Study: Compassionate Care — Autism Care Practice Website

## The Client

A private practice providing **home and community-based care** for adolescents and adults with autism, operating in the local area. The practice was growing through referrals but had no website — a critical gap when insurance providers and families research care options online before making contact.

## The Problem

- No web presence at all — potential clients couldn't find or vet the practice online
- Referral-only growth model was limiting capacity
- No way to showcase services, credentials, or approach to care
- Competitors with websites were capturing search traffic first
- Parents and caregivers needed a trustworthy, professional first impression — a bare minimum for this industry

## What We Built

A clean, accessible, SEO-optimized website featuring:

- **Accessibility-first design** — ARIA-compliant, high contrast, keyboard navigable (especially important for a practice serving individuals with disabilities)
- **Services showcase** — clear breakdown of all six service offerings (In-Home Support, Community Integration, Life Skills Training, Communication Support, Social Skills Development, Family Collaboration)
- **Trust signals** — credentials, approach to care, and testimonial section
- **Contact form** — integrated lead capture for new client inquiries
- **SEO structure** — semantic HTML5, meta descriptions, fast load times for local search ranking
- **Mobile responsive** — parents researching on phones can navigate and contact easily

## The Results

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| No website | Professional, accessible site live |
| Referral-only growth | Web-based inquiry form capturing leads |
| Not findable in search | SEO-optimized for local care searches |
| No service clarity online | Full service breakdown with descriptions |
| No online trust signals | Credentials, testimonials, professional design |

## Why It Matters

The healthcare and care services industry runs on **trust**. A well-designed, accessible website isn't just a nice-to-have — it's table stakes. Insurance providers, case managers, and families all do online research before committing. Without a site, this practice was losing potential clients at the first step of the funnel.

## Tech Stack

- HTML5 / CSS3 (lightweight, fast-loading — no framework overhead needed)
- Vanilla JavaScript (smooth scroll, form handling)
- Formspree-compatible contact form
- Zero dependencies — blazing fast load times

**1 week** from brief to deployment-ready.
