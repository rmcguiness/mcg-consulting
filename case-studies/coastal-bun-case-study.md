# Case Study: The Coastal Bun — Food Truck Website + Online Ordering

## The Client

**The Coastal Bun** is an Asian fusion bao bun food truck operating in the Florida Keys — one of the highest-traffic tourism corridors in the Southeast US. The business is run by a single owner who needed a professional online presence to compete with established restaurants in the area, but had no website or digital marketing footprint.

## The Problem

- No website or online ordering capability
- Zero visibility in local search results
- Customers had no way to find the truck's schedule or location
- No way to accept payments online — cash/card only at the truck
- Competing against restaurants with full websites, Google listings, and social media

## What We Built

A full-stack Next.js 15 website with:

- **Mobile-first design** — 60%+ of Keys tourism traffic comes from phones
- **Square payment integration** — online ordering built in, zero extra fees beyond Square's standard rate
- **Location & schedule tracker** — customers know exactly where and when to find the truck
- **SEO-optimized structure** — structured data, sitemap, meta tags for local search ranking
- **Analytics-ready** — Google Analytics + Facebook Pixel wired up and ready to activate
- **Menu showcase** — high-quality food photography with clear pricing
- **Contact & booking forms** — catering inquiries and event bookings

## The Results

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| No website | Full Next.js site live on custom domain |
| No online ordering | Square-integrated checkout |
| Not findable online | SEO-optimized for local search |
| Schedule shared verbally | Live location + schedule page |
| No data on customers | Analytics + pixel tracking ready |

## Why It Matters

The Florida Keys tourism market is worth billions annually, but most small food & beverage businesses have weak or nonexistent web presence. A single well-built site with online ordering can capture customers who are planning trips in advance — the highest-value segment. The Coastal Bun went from invisible to a searchable, bookable, payable business in under two weeks.

## Tech Stack

- Next.js 15 (App Router)
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- Square Payments API
- Vercel deployment

## Timeline

**2 weeks** from brief to live site.
