# Case Study: Islamorada Sport Fishing — Charter Booking Website

## The Client

A family-owned sport fishing charter operating out of Islamorada — the self-proclaimed "Sport Fishing Capital of the World." With decades of local knowledge, a fleet of fully rigged offshore boats, and a loyal base of repeat customers, the business was thriving in peak season. The problem: every booking still happened by phone or in person at the marina.

## The Problem

- No website — the charter was completely invisible to online search
- All bookings required a phone call or showing up at the dock
- Competitors with booking sites were capturing the "advance planner" tourist segment
- No way to showcase trip types, pricing, gear, or catch history online
- Peak season overwhelmed staff with booking calls that could be self-served
- No Google Business profile, no reviews platform, no social proof

## What We Built

A conversion-focused charter booking website:

- **FareHarbor booking integration** — customers select date, trip type, group size, and pay a deposit in under 3 minutes
- **Trip catalog with clear pricing** — half-day inshore, full-day offshore, private and shared charters
- **Catch gallery** — high-res photos of real catches (mahi, sailfish, tarpon, snook) as social proof
- **Local SEO structure** — optimized for "Islamorada fishing charter," "Keys offshore fishing"
- **Mobile-first design** — over 65% of charter searches happen on mobile
- **Google Business integration** — coordinated with Google Business Profile setup

## The Results

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| Bookings by phone or walk-in only | 40% of bookings now online |
| No website — invisible to search | Page 1 Google for "Islamorada fishing charter" |
| No online trip showcase | Full catalog: half-day, full-day, private, group |
| Zero photo presence online | High-res gallery showing catches & experiences |
| Staff overwhelmed by booking calls | Self-serve booking with real-time availability |

## Key Metrics

- **+40%** bookings shifted online within 90 days
- **#1** local search result for target keywords
- **2 weeks** from kickoff to live site
- **65%+** mobile traffic share, fully optimized
