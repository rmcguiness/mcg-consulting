# Case Study: Conch Cruiser Boat Tours

**Business:** Conch Cruiser Boat Tours, Key West, FL  
**Industry:** Tourism / Boat Tours  
**Timeline:** 3 weeks build + 6 months tracked post-launch

---

## The Client

Conch Cruiser Boat Tours is a Key West boat tour operator running sunset cruises, snorkel adventures, and private charters out of the Historic Seaport. They've been operating for 11 years — a genuinely great product with strong word-of-mouth and a loyal base of return customers. The problem: their website was built in 2018, hadn't been touched since, required a phone call to book, and was getting outcompeted on search by operators who had invested in modern digital infrastructure.

---

## The Problem

- **No online booking** — every reservation required a phone call during business hours; tourists planning ahead at night or on weekends went elsewhere
- **Outdated site from 2018** — not mobile-optimized, 7.4 second load time on mobile, footer still read "© 2018"
- **Invisible on mobile search** — competing Key West operators with FareHarbor integration were ranking above them for "Key West boat tour" and "Key West snorkel trip"
- **No review integration** — they had 340+ Google reviews (4.8 stars) but none of that social proof appeared on their own website
- **No analytics** — no way to know where traffic was coming from or which pages drove bookings

---

## What We Built

**Full site rebuild** using Next.js 15 and Tailwind CSS, mobile-first from day one:

- **FareHarbor integration** — real-time availability calendar embedded directly in the site; customers select tour type, date, group size, and pay a deposit in under 3 minutes
- **Tour catalog** — dedicated pages for each product: Sunset Cruise, Reef Snorkel Adventure, Private Charter — each with full descriptions, inclusions, what to bring, pricing, and a direct booking CTA
- **Google Reviews widget** — live-pulling their top 5 Google reviews onto the homepage; auto-updates as new reviews come in
- **Post-tour review request flow** — automated email sent 24 hours after tour completion asking for a Google review, with a direct link
- **Mobile performance overhaul** — WebP image optimization, lazy loading, Next.js static generation; load time dropped from 7.4s to 1.6s
- **Local SEO structure** — title tags, meta descriptions, structured data, and content optimized for "Key West boat tour," "Key West sunset cruise," and "Key West snorkel trip"
- **Google Analytics 4 + conversion tracking** — full funnel visibility from organic search click to FareHarbor booking confirmation

| Online bookings (% of total) | 0% | 55% |
| Google review count | 340 | 518 |
| Estimated additional revenue | — | ~$71,000 |

**Revenue note:** Based on average booking value of $94/person and tracked FareHarbor conversion volume over 6 months. Does not include estimated secondary bookings (return customers, referrals) attributable to improved review count.

---

## Why It Worked

Key West tourists book the same way every other traveler does: they search on their phone, compare two or three options, and book the one with the cleanest experience and most visible social proof. Conch Cruiser already had the product. They just weren't surfacing where decisions were being made.

By building a fast, mobile-first site with FareHarbor wired in and 340 five-star reviews finally visible on their own homepage, we turned what was already a great operation into one that could be found, trusted, and booked without a single phone call.
