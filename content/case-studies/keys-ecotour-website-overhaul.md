## The Client

Coastal Drift Kayak Tours runs half-day kayak excursions, sunset paddleboard tours, and guided snorkel trips through the mangroves and backcountry flats around Key West. Owner-operator Dana Whitfield started the company nine years ago with two sit-on-top kayaks and a van. Today she runs six guides, a fleet of 24 kayaks, and three rigid inflatable boats for snorkel groups.

Her tours get exceptional reviews — 4.8 stars across 290+ Google and TripAdvisor reviews. Guests consistently mention Dana's guides by name. Return customer rate runs around 30%, which is high for the eco-tour market where most business is one-time visitors.

The problem wasn't the experience. The problem was the front door.

---

## The Problem

Dana's website was hand-coded HTML. Literally. She'd paid a local guy $800 to build it in 2018, and it hadn't been touched since. By the time she came to MCG, the site was doing real damage:

- **No online booking, anywhere.** Every reservation required a phone call or a Facebook message. The inquiry form on her contact page had a broken submit button for at least eight months. She didn't know — no one told her, they just left.
- **Mobile completely broken** — the layout collapsed on any screen smaller than a laptop. Menu links overlapped. The hero image pushed the text off-screen. On an iPhone, you couldn't see the tour descriptions at all.
- **No prices visible** — Dana had deliberately left pricing off the old site because she was worried about sticker shock. The result: every inquiry started with a price question, and a significant percentage dropped off before booking.
- **Invisible on Google** — searches for "kayak tours Key West," "mangrove kayak Key West," and "snorkel tours Key West" returned results from OTAs (Viator, TripAdvisor) and one well-optimized competitor. Coastal Drift didn't appear in the top 30.
- **Phone-dependent scheduling** — Dana and her office manager were spending a combined 6–8 hours per week on booking calls, availability questions, and "is the tour still running if it rains?" texts. That's time not spent on guide training, equipment maintenance, or anything else that actually runs the business.

When she looked at her Google Analytics — which hadn't been checked in over a year — she found a mobile bounce rate of 76%. Three out of four mobile visitors left immediately. They weren't even reading the page.

---

## What We Built

**Full site rebuild** — built mobile-first, fast, and designed around one goal: get visitors from landing page to confirmed booking without friction.

- **FareHarbor booking integration** — every tour page has a live availability calendar embedded directly in the page. Visitors select a tour, pick a date, choose their group size, and pay a deposit. From landing on the site to confirmed booking: under four minutes. No phone call. No contact form. No waiting.
- **Dedicated tour pages** — separate pages for each tour type: half-day kayak, sunset paddleboard, mangrove snorkel, and private charters. Each page includes honest descriptions of what to expect, difficulty level, what's included, minimum age requirements, seasonal notes, and transparent pricing. No surprises.
- **Pricing up front** — we pushed Dana to publish her prices. She was nervous about it. Her half-day kayak tour starts at $79/person, which is competitive for Key West. Showing the price filtered out low-intent browsers and significantly improved booking conversion from visitors who stayed. 
- **Review integration** — Google and TripAdvisor reviews pulled and displayed on the homepage and individual tour pages. 290+ reviews at 4.8 stars. Visible immediately, updated automatically.
- **Real photography** — the old site used three stock photos from a beach somewhere in Hawaii. We built the new site around Dana's actual tour photos: mangrove tunnels, guests in kayaks, dolphins spotted off the bow of the snorkel boat, sunrise shots over the flats. Every image optimized and WebP-converted for mobile load performance.
- **Mobile performance overhaul** — load time dropped from 9.4 seconds (on mobile) to 2.1 seconds. Lighthouse mobile score: 22 → 88. The layout works on every screen, from a 2019 iPhone SE to a current Android.
- **Local SEO foundation** — structured data (LocalBusiness + TouristAttraction + TouristTrip schema), optimized title tags and meta descriptions, content targeting the specific phrases tourists search: "kayak tours Key West," "mangrove tour Key West," "snorkel tours Key West FL." Google Business Profile updated and synced.
- **Automated post-trip review request** — FareHarbor triggers an email 24 hours after each completed tour with a one-click link to leave a Google review. Simple, non-pushy, effective.

## Why It Worked

Eco-tour and water sports operators in Key West are fighting on two fronts: the major OTAs (Viator, TripAdvisor, GetYourGuide) that eat margin on every booking, and the handful of direct competitors with functioning websites. A good direct-booking site wins on both fronts — you pay no commission, and you control the guest relationship from the first click.

Dana already had everything a tourist wants: well-reviewed tours, experienced guides, and a genuinely great product. The missing piece was a site that could show that to someone searching at 10pm in a Cincinnati hotel room, trying to plan tomorrow's activity before they go to sleep.

The site didn't need to be fancy. It needed to load fast, show real photos, display honest prices, prove social trust through reviews, and let someone book right now.

That's what we built.
