## The Client

Backcountry Keys Charters runs flats, backcountry, and bridge fishing trips out of Marathon, FL — one of the best fisheries in the Keys for tarpon, permit, and bonefish. Captain Ray has been guiding for 14 years. He knows the water better than most people know their commute. His repeat client rate is over 60%.

The problem wasn't the fishing. The problem was that first-time customers couldn't find him, couldn't book him, and couldn't tell he was any good — all because his website was built in 2017 and hadn't been touched since.

---

## The Problem

- **Phone-only booking** — every trip required a call or text during business hours. Tourists browsing at 11pm on a Wednesday night from Ohio couldn't book. They'd find someone who let them.
- **Outdated site** — built on a WordPress theme that hadn't been updated in four years. Mobile load time: 8.2 seconds. Layout broke on newer phones. Footer still said "© 2019."
- **No social proof on-site** — Captain Ray had 180+ Google reviews at 4.9 stars, but none of them appeared on his website. Visitors had to go find them separately.
- **Invisible on search** — competitors with modern sites and FareHarbor integration were outranking him for "Marathon fishing charter," "backcountry fishing Marathon FL," and "Keys flats fishing guide."
- **Weekend booking gap** — Friday through Sunday accounted for 70% of tourist inquiry calls, but Captain Ray was on the water and couldn't answer. Those callers booked elsewhere.

---

## What We Built

**Full site rebuild** — mobile-first, fast, built to convert:

- **FareHarbor booking integration** — real-time availability calendar on every trip page. Customers pick their trip type (flats, backcountry, bridge), select a date, choose half-day or full-day, and pay a deposit. Total time from landing on the site to confirmed booking: under 3 minutes.
- **Trip pages with substance** — dedicated pages for each trip type with honest descriptions: what you'll target, what tackle is provided, what to bring, seasonal notes, and pricing. No stock photos — all real catches from Captain Ray's trips.
- **Google Reviews widget** — top reviews pulled live onto the homepage and trip pages. Updated automatically. 4.9 stars and 180+ reviews visible the moment someone lands on the site.
- **Photo gallery** — organized by species and trip type. Optimized WebP images, lazy-loaded, so the page doesn't choke on mobile. Tourists want to see the fish — now they can without waiting 8 seconds.
- **Mobile performance overhaul** — load time dropped from 8.2 seconds to 1.9 seconds. Lighthouse mobile score went from 34 to 91.
- **Local SEO foundation** — proper title tags, meta descriptions, structured data (LocalBusiness + TouristTrip schema), and content built around the terms tourists actually search: "Marathon fishing charter," "backcountry fishing Florida Keys," "flats fishing guide Marathon."
- **Post-trip review flow** — automated email 24 hours after each trip with a direct Google review link. Simple, one-tap.

**Revenue impact:** Based on Captain Ray's average trip rate of $550 (half-day) and the 40% increase in monthly bookings, the site generated an estimated **$14,850 in additional revenue** over 90 days — net of the cost of the rebuild.

## Why It Worked

Marathon fishing charters compete on two things: the captain's reputation and the ease of booking. Captain Ray already had the reputation — 14 years on the water, 4.9 stars, clients who rebook every season. But none of that was visible or accessible to a first-time tourist searching on their phone at 11pm.

The site didn't need to be flashy. It needed to be fast, bookable, and trustworthy. FareHarbor handles the booking. The reviews handle the trust. The speed and SEO handle the visibility. Captain Ray handles the fishing.
