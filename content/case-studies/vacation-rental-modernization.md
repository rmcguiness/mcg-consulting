## The Client

Horizon Keys Rentals manages 38 vacation rental properties across Islamorada and the Upper Keys — waterfront homes, canal-front condos, and a handful of estate-level properties that book $600–$900/night in peak season. Owner Mike Devereaux has been in Keys property management for 14 years. His team handles everything: guest communication, cleaning turnover, maintenance, owner reporting.

His occupancy rates are strong — 78% annually, higher in winter. Guest reviews average 4.7 stars. Repeat guest rate is around 25%, which is solid for a market where most visitors are one-timers.

The product wasn't the problem. The margin was.

---

## The Problem

Mike's website was built on LiveRez, a vacation rental platform that was current in 2016 but hasn't aged well. The front end ran on Drupal with a design that looked like it belonged to that era. More critically, the site wasn't generating direct bookings — so the majority of reservations came through Airbnb and VRBO, each taking a 15–20% service fee on every booking.

The math was brutal:

- **38 properties, average nightly rate $420, average 18 bookings/month across the portfolio**
- **At 20% platform commission: roughly $5,600/month going to Airbnb and VRBO** — money that could have stayed in Mike's business if guests booked direct
- **No mobile optimization** — the LiveRez/Drupal site rendered poorly on phones. Property photos were slow to load, search filters didn't work on mobile, and the booking flow required desktop-sized screens to complete
- **Invisible on Google for direct searches** — "Islamorada vacation rentals," "Upper Keys rental homes," "waterfront rental Islamorada" — all returned Airbnb, VRBO, and Vacasa in the top spots. Horizon Keys didn't appear in the first three pages
- **No structured data** — Google's vacation rental search features pull from schema markup. Mike's site had none, which meant his properties never appeared in Google's vacation rental pack
- **Outdated property pages** — no virtual tours, no neighborhood context, no interactive calendars. Each listing was a photo gallery with a paragraph and a "call to inquire" button

Mike knew he was overpaying the platforms. He just didn't have a site that could compete with them.

---

## What We Built

**Full site rebuild** — designed to do one thing: convert search traffic into direct bookings without Airbnb or VRBO in the middle.

- **Direct booking engine** — integrated with Lodgify for real-time availability, instant booking, and secure payment processing. Guests search by date, location, property type, and guest count — and book without leaving the site. No phone call, no inquiry form, no 24-hour wait for a reply.
- **Google Vacation Rental schema markup** — every property page includes structured data (VacationRental + LodgingBusiness schema) so Google can surface listings in its vacation rental search pack. Property type, location, amenities, nightly rate, availability — all machine-readable.
- **Mobile-first design** — 72% of Horizon Keys' traffic comes from mobile. The new site loads property pages in under 2.5 seconds on a phone. Photo galleries swipe natively. The booking flow works on any screen size without pinching or zooming.
- **Property pages that sell** — each listing includes high-res photo galleries, an interactive availability calendar, neighborhood details (distance to boat ramps, restaurants, dive shops), amenity lists, and transparent pricing with no hidden fees. Guests know exactly what they're booking before they click "Reserve."
- **"Book Direct & Save" messaging** — every property page shows a price comparison: "This property on Airbnb: $504/night. Book direct: $420/night. You save $84." Straightforward, honest, effective.
- **Local SEO buildout** — optimized title tags and content targeting the phrases tourists actually search: "Islamorada vacation rentals," "Upper Keys waterfront rental," "Florida Keys house rental with dock." Google Business Profile updated with property portfolio and booking links.
- **Automated review collection** — post-checkout emails request a Google review with a one-click link. Simple and non-aggressive.

**Revenue impact:** The shift from 18% direct bookings to 52% direct bookings moved roughly 6 additional bookings per month off-platform. At an average commission of $80–$100 per booking, that's **$4,800/month in fees that now stay with Horizon Keys** instead of going to Airbnb and VRBO. Over the 90-day tracking window: **$14,400 in recovered margin.**

The site build paid for itself in under 30 days.

## Why It Worked

Vacation rental managers in the Keys are caught in a trap: the platforms drive bookings, but they take 15–20% of every one. Most managers accept it because their own websites can't compete — they load slow, look dated, and don't show up on Google.

The fix isn't complicated. It's a site that loads fast, shows real photos, displays transparent pricing, lets guests book instantly, and tells Google exactly what each property is through structured data. When a tourist searches "Islamorada waterfront rental" at 10pm and finds a property they can book in three minutes without creating an Airbnb account — they book direct.

Mike's properties, pricing, and service didn't change. What changed was that guests could find and book him without a middleman.
