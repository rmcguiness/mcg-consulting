## The Client

Keys Aqua Sports runs jet ski rentals, guided jet ski tours, parasailing flights, and paddleboard rentals out of Marathon, FL — right on the Overseas Highway where tourist traffic is heaviest. Owner-operator Mike Serrano has been running watersports trips in the Keys for eleven years. His repeat customer rate sits around 35%, which is strong for a segment where most customers are one-time vacationers.

His TripAdvisor reviews are excellent — 4.7 stars across 210+ reviews. Guests consistently mention the crew by name. The parasailing operation is one of only three in the Middle Keys.

The problem wasn't the experience. The problem was that nobody could find it, and if they did, they couldn't book it.

---

## The Problem

Mike's website had been limping along on a shared hosting plan since 2016. By the time he contacted MCG, the situation was critical:

- **Site completely down** — the hosting had lapsed and the domain was returning connection errors. For at least three weeks, every tourist searching "jet ski rental Marathon FL" got a dead link. Mike didn't realize it until a repeat customer texted him asking if he'd closed down.
- **No online booking** — even when the site was live, every rental required a phone call or a walk-up. Tourists browsing from their hotel at 10pm couldn't reserve a jet ski for the next morning. They'd find an operator who let them.
- **Mobile completely broken** — the old site used absolute positioning and fixed-width layouts from the early 2010s. On any phone newer than 2018, the menu overlapped the hero image, the pricing table ran off-screen, and the "Call to Book" button was hidden behind the footer.
- **No trust signals** — Mike's 210+ TripAdvisor reviews, his Coast Guard certifications, and his eleven years of operation were invisible on the site. A first-time visitor had no reason to trust the business over a competitor with a cleaner page.
- **Invisible on Google** — searches for "jet ski rental Marathon," "parasailing Marathon FL," and "watersports Florida Keys" returned competitors and OTA listings. Keys Aqua Sports didn't appear in the top 20.

The revenue leak was significant. Mike estimated he was missing 8–12 bookings per week during peak season — customers who searched, found nothing or a broken page, and booked with someone else.

---

## What We Built

**Full site rebuild** — mobile-first, fast, and built around one goal: get a tourist from Google to a confirmed booking without friction.

- **FareHarbor booking integration** — every activity page (jet ski rental, jet ski tour, parasailing, paddleboard) has a live availability calendar. Visitors pick their activity, select a date and time slot, choose their group size, and pay a deposit. Total time from landing to confirmed booking: under three minutes. The system captures bookings 24/7 — including the after-hours window (6pm–9am) that Mike was previously losing entirely.
- **Activity pages with substance** — dedicated pages for each activity with honest details: duration, what's included, age and weight requirements, what to wear, seasonal availability, and transparent pricing. No stock photos — all real shots from Mike's trips and flights.
- **Safety credentials front and center** — Coast Guard certification, commercial vessel inspection, and insurance documentation displayed prominently on the homepage and every activity page. For parasailing especially, safety credentials are a top decision factor for families.
- **Review integration** — TripAdvisor and Google reviews pulled live onto the homepage and activity pages. 210+ reviews at 4.7 stars, visible immediately.
- **Mobile performance overhaul** — load time dropped from completely broken to 2.0 seconds. Lighthouse mobile score: 89. The layout works on every screen size, from older iPhones to current Androids.
- **Photo and video gallery** — organized by activity type. Parasailing aerial shots, jet ski action photos, paddleboard sunset shots. All optimized WebP, lazy-loaded. Tourists want to see what they're buying — now they can without waiting.
- **Local SEO foundation** — structured data (LocalBusiness + TouristTrip schema), optimized title tags and meta descriptions, content targeting the exact phrases tourists search: "jet ski rental Marathon FL," "parasailing Marathon Florida Keys," "watersports Marathon Key." Google Business Profile updated and synced with the new site.

## Why It Worked

Watersports rentals in the Keys are an impulse purchase for most tourists. They're on vacation, it's a sunny day, they want to do something on the water. The decision window is short — they'll search on their phone, look at two or three options, and book the one that makes it easiest.

Mike already had the fleet, the crew, the reviews, and the location. What he didn't have was a functioning front door. A dead website isn't just a missed opportunity — it actively signals to tourists that the business might be closed, unsafe, or unreliable.

The site didn't need to be complicated. It needed to exist, load fast, show real photos, prove safety credentials, and let someone book right now — even at 11pm from their Airbnb.
