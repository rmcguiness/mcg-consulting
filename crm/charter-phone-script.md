# MCG Consulting — Fishing Charter Phone Pitch Script

**Use when calling:** Strip Strike Charters, Right On Charters, Couple-A-Bucks, Snorkel the Keys, etc.

---

## Opening (5-10 seconds)

"Hey [Captain Name], this is Ryan McGuiness calling from MCG Consulting down here in the Keys. Got a quick minute?"

*If yes:* "Great, I'll keep it short — I work with fishing charter captains on their websites and booking systems, and I noticed something on yours I wanted to flag."

*If bad time:* "No problem at all. What's a better time to catch you — early morning before you head out, or after you're back in?"

---

## The Hook (20 seconds — tailored by business)

**Strip Strike / Right On / general charters:**
"Your boat and your reputation are clearly top-notch, but your website's booking flow is costing you trips. Right now, if someone finds you on Google at 10pm Friday night — which is peak booking time for weekend trips — they can't book without calling or emailing. By Monday morning they've booked someone else."

**Right On Charters specifically:**
"Your Book Now button isn't connected to anything. Every click on that button is a customer you're losing to a charter that has online booking set up."

**Couple-A-Bucks:**
"I had trouble finding your contact info — a lot of potential customers are going to give up before they find you. A quick fix there alone would probably put a few more trips on your calendar."

---

## The Offer (15 seconds)

"What I do is build fishing charter websites that are actually set up to book trips — connected to FareHarbor or a similar system, loaded with your customer reviews, and optimized to show up when tourists search for charters in [location].

Most captains I work with see an extra 3-5 bookings a month just from removing the friction on their site. At $400-600 a trip, that pays for itself fast."

---

## The Ask

"I'd love to do a free 20-minute review of your current site and show you exactly what I'd change. No sales pressure — if it's not worth your time after 20 minutes, no hard feelings. What do you think?"

---

## Common Objections

**"I already have a website."**
"I know, and it's a good start — but having a website and having a website that books trips are two different things. I can show you specifically what's keeping tourists from converting."

**"I get plenty of word-of-mouth business."**
"Word-of-mouth is the best kind — no argument there. But most charter captains I talk to have a waitlist for regulars and empty slots for new customers. Your site is how you fill those empty slots automatically, even when you're out on the water."

**"How much does it cost?"**
"Typically $3,500-5,000 for a full build — depends on what you need. But I'd rather show you the audit first and let you decide if it's worth it. Most guys see the ROI in the first season."

**"I don't have time for this."**
"That's exactly why I handle everything — you just answer a few questions and send me your photos. Takes maybe an hour of your time total. I do the rest."

**"I tried a web guy before and it was a headache."**
"Yeah, that's common. The difference is I specifically work with charters in the Keys — I know your business, your customers, and exactly what a fishing charter site needs. It's not a generic template."

---

## Close

"Great — let me send you a quick 5-minute audit of your site so you can see what I'm talking about before we talk. What's your email? I'll have it over today."

*Or if they're skeptical:*
"No pressure at all. Here's my number — if you ever look at your site on a Saturday morning and wonder why the phone isn't ringing, give me a call: [phone number]."

---

## After the Call

- Email the free audit within 24 hours (use the /audit page on mcg-consulting site)
- Reference the specific weakness you mentioned on the call
- Sign off: "Looking forward to helping you fill more trips — Ryan"

---
