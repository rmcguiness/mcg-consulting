# Fishing Charter Objection Handling — Quick Reference

For Ryan's charter captain outreach calls. Keep it honest, keep it short.

---

## "I don't have time to deal with a website redesign."

You don't have to. We handle everything — design, content, booking setup, the whole build. All we need from you is a 30-minute kickoff call and access to your photos. Most captains give us one phone call and then don't hear from us again until we show them the finished site. You stay on the water.

---

## "My customers find me through word of mouth / I'm already booked up."

That's great — and it means you've earned a reputation worth protecting. But word-of-mouth clients Google you before they call. If what they find is an outdated site with no reviews and no way to book, some of them quietly go elsewhere. And if you ever want to raise your rates, fill shoulder-season gaps, or attract new premium clients, your website is how they'll find you.

---

## "I had a bad experience with a web company before."

You're not the first captain to tell me that. A lot of web companies sell a template, disappear, and leave you with something you can't update. We're local, we specialize in Keys tourism businesses, and we don't ghost. I'll give you references from other charter captains we've worked with. And we don't ask for full payment upfront — you see the site before you pay the balance.

---

## "How do I know this will actually get me more bookings?"

Fair question. Here's a real example: a Marathon backcountry charter captain we worked with went from phone-only booking to 40% more trips per month after we rebuilt his site with FareHarbor integration. Nine extra bookings a month. The biggest driver? Tourists could book at 10pm from their hotel instead of playing phone tag. I can share the full case study if you want to see the numbers.

---

## "I can't afford it right now."

The site costs $3,500 to build. If it recovers just two extra bookings a month at your trip rate — and based on what we're seeing with your current site, that's conservative — it pays for itself in under four months. After that it's pure profit. We also offer an all-in annual package at $5,500 that spreads the investment across the year and includes ongoing maintenance. Either way, the question isn't whether you can afford the site — it's how many bookings you're losing without one.
