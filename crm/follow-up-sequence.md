## Touch 2: Short Follow-Up — Local Context Hook
**Timing:** Day 4 (after initial email)
**Subject:** Spring break traffic is coming

Hey [Captain Name],

Quick follow-up on my email earlier this week. Spring break starts hitting the Keys in a couple weeks — that's your highest-traffic window of the year for new customers searching online.

A site that can't book trips at 11pm on a Tuesday night is leaving money on the dock, especially when every hotel in the Keys is full of tourists with phones in their hands.

If you want a second pair of eyes on your site before the rush, I'm happy to take a quick look — no charge, no commitment. Just figured the timing matters.

Ryan McGuiness
MCG Consulting

---

## Touch 3: Final Value-Add — Free Audit Offer
**Timing:** Day 10 (after initial email)
**Subject:** Free site audit — no strings

[Captain Name],

Last note from me. I put together a full website audit for charters and tour operators like yours — the kind of thing we normally charge for. I'd like to do yours for free.

It covers what's working, what's costing you bookings, and what a fix would actually look like. Takes us about a day to put together, and you'd own the report whether we ever work together or not.

If you're interested, just reply "sure" and I'll get it started. If not, no worries at all — I'll get out of your inbox.

Either way, tight lines this season.

Ryan McGuiness
MCG Consulting

---

## Sequence Notes

- **If they reply to any touch:** Stop the sequence, move to live conversation.
- **If no reply after Touch 3:** Mark as "Nurture" in CRM. Revisit in 30 days with a seasonal angle (lobster mini-season, tarpon run, summer tourism wave).
- **Couple-A-Bucks & Snorkel the Keys:** No email on file. Find contact via Google Business listing, Yelp, or use website contact form for Touch 1. If using a form, keep it even shorter.
