# SEGMENT A — ART GALLERIES

## Pitch Framework: Galleries

**Core value props:**
1. **Google visibility** — tourists search "art gallery [town]" before they arrive; Facebook pages don't rank
2. **Online sales** — prints, merchandise, and commissions can sell 24/7; Facebook can't take payments
3. **Artist referral network** — co-op galleries are hubs; one happy gallery owner opens the door to 10+ individual artists
4. **Low barrier** — $3,500 starting price is often less than a single painting or framing job

**Tone:** Respectful of the art, not condescending about the tech. Lead with the business opportunity, not "your website is bad."

# SEGMENT B — WEDDING PLANNERS & VENUES

## Pitch Framework: Wedding Planners

**Core value props:**
1. **Booking conversion** — destination couples research extensively online; no website = no consideration
2. **Seasonal photo updates** — a website that showcases the current season's real weddings builds ongoing social proof
3. **Booking/inquiry integration** — consultation request forms capture leads automatically; Facebook DMs get lost
4. **Third-party independence** — WeddingWire/TheKnot charge commissions and control the experience; owning your website means owning your leads
5. **High ROI** — one additional booking from organic search ($2,000–$5,000 planning fee) exceeds the cost of a $3,500 site

**Tone:** Peer-to-peer, not sales-y. Acknowledge their reputation and experience first. Make them feel like the expert — because they are. Frame the website as a tool that does the selling so they don't have to.

## General Notes
- **Gallery emails:** Keep subject lines gallery/art-specific. Don't use web dev jargon ("SEO," "UX," "responsive"). Use "show up in Google," "tourists can find you," "shows your work."
- **Wedding emails:** Acknowledge reputation first. Planners are proud of their craft — lead with a compliment that's specific and true (reviews, years in business). The tech ask comes second.
- **Phone calls:** Gallery owners often prefer phone. For galleries, call first if possible — email as follow-up.
- **Best send times:** Tues–Thurs, 9–11am Eastern. Avoid Mondays (busy) and Fridays (winding down).
- **Opt-out:** If no response after Touch 3, move to "nurture" list — invite to follow on social, add to quarterly newsletter list.
- **Referral ask:** Always include referral ask in Touch 3. Both gallery and wedding segments have tight-knit vendor communities.
