## Gallery Pitch Framework

**Core insight from research:** These galleries aren't "behind on technology" — they're successful local businesses that simply haven't had the time or the right offer. The pitch isn't "your website is bad." It's "here's specific business you're already missing."

**3 hook angles that work for galleries:**
1. **The search gap** — tourists search before they drive; Facebook and dead domains don't show up
2. **The artist story** — every gallery has a human story that sells better than any listing; a website is where that story lives
3. **The competitor angle** — gently surface that a gallery two miles away IS findable, and that's where walk-ins go first

# SEGMENT B — WEDDING VENUES & PLANNERS

## Wedding Pitch Framework

**Core insight from research:** Destination wedding couples plan 12–18 months out and research extensively online. A planner or venue with no website loses bookings to competitors with mediocre websites. The pitch is: "every week your site is weak is a week you're not getting discovered by couples currently in the research phase."

**3 hook angles for wedding businesses:**
1. **The research window** — brides are searching right NOW; March–May is when fall/winter weddings get booked
2. **The trust gap** — third-party profiles (WeddingWire, The Knot) are discovery tools, not conversion tools; a personal website closes the deal
3. **The experience gap** — destination couples need to fall in love with a vendor before they call; a portfolio site does that; a directory profile doesn't
