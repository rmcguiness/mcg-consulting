# SEGMENT B — MARINA OPERATORS

## Pitch Framework: Marinas

**Core value props:**
1. **Slip availability calendar** — a boater planning a Keys cruise calls once. If nobody answers, they go elsewhere. A live availability calendar converts that call into a self-service booking
2. **Online reservations** — transient dockage is a same-week decision for most cruising boaters. A "Reserve a Slip" button on mobile closes deals in real time
3. **Fuel pricing transparency** — many marinas don't post fuel prices online. Boaters actively seek this out to plan their cruise budget. Being the marina that posts prices = trust + traffic
4. **Google Maps / local SEO** — when a boater searches "marina Key Largo" or "overnight dockage Marathon FL" while planning, the marinas with good websites and GMB profiles appear first
5. **Multi-service packaging** — marinas with restaurants, bait shops, launch services, and vacation rentals have massive cross-sell potential that a website can present as a complete package

**Tone:** Practical and direct. Marina operators are no-nonsense businesspeople. Skip the fluff. Lead with the specific gap and the dollar-value of fixing it.
