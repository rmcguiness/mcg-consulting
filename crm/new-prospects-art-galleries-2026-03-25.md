**Research Date:** 2026-03-25
**Sources:** keysarts.com directory audit, marathonflorida.com artists directory, Islamorada Chamber, direct site checks, Wayback Machine, Google search
**Prior Coverage:** See new-prospects-galleries-2026-03-22.md (covers Key Largo Art Gallery, Guild Hall Gallery, Artists in Paradise, Michelle Nicole Lowe, 7 Artists & Friends, Island Iron & Ink, Kalypso Gallery, Morada Way Arts District, FL Keys Watercolor Society)
**Focus:** Islamorada / Morada Way district + Tavernier corridor — a parallel cluster not fully covered in the March 22 batch

---

## Research Summary

This batch focuses on the **Islamorada / Tavernier gallery corridor** (MM 82–88.8) and newly identified Key Largo-area leads. Direct website checks revealed a striking pattern: several well-known galleries have either dead/expired domains or websites that are 10+ years old. The Morada Way Arts District (Islamorada) is the epicenter — a dense tourist destination with multiple HOT leads within walking distance of each other. A single sale in this corridor creates natural word-of-mouth to neighboring studios.

## Outreach Notes

**Cluster strategy:** Prospects #1–#5 are all within the MM 81–88 corridor of Islamorada. A single trip through the area would allow in-person drop-ins after initial email contact. Frame it as "we work with several businesses in the Morada Way area" to establish local credibility.

**Expiry/dead domain angle:** Works well for Gallery of the Arts and John Hawver — these prospects know they NEED a website (they either had one or are established enough to know better). The conversation is "let's fix what's broken," not "here's why you need a site."

**Adjacent referrals:** Old Road Gallery → Redbone Gallery → Gallery of the Arts is a natural conversation chain. One good relationship in the Morada Way district opens all the doors on that street.
