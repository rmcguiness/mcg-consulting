# Dive Shop Prospects — Florida Keys
**Research Date:** 2026-03-24
**Source:** Direct site audits, TripAdvisor, Google, visitfloridakeys.com, tripadvisor.com, yelp.com
**Segment:** Dive Shops & Scuba Operators
**Fit:** MCG Consulting web builds $3,500–7,500 + $500/mo maintenance

---

## Research Summary

Key Largo is branded the "Dive Capital of the World" and anchors the Keys dive market — the highest concentration of dive operators in the continental US. The segment splits sharply in two: (1) Award-winning shops with modern websites and FareHarbor/SmartWaiver booking integrations (Key Dives, Key Largo Dive Center, Horizon Divers) and (2) longtime operators coasting on reputation with embarrassingly dated or nearly empty websites. The second group is the MCG sweet spot.

The core pitch for dive shops is discoverable online booking. A tourist from Ohio decides to scuba dive while planning a Keys trip. They Google "scuba diving Key Largo" on their laptop a week out. Whichever shops appear with clean sites and an easy "Book Now" button capture that decision in the moment. Shops with 2007 HTML sites or blank WordPress pages lose that tourist to a competitor — even if their actual service is superior.

This segment has strong economics: a 2-tank reef dive runs $80–120/person, PADI certification courses run $350–600+, and equipment rentals and retail gear add margin. A shop that converts even 5 additional bookings/month from web traffic covers MCG's $500 maintenance fee in the first week.
