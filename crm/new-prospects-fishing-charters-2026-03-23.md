## Research Summary

The Florida Keys fishing charter market has two tiers online:

**Tier A — Multi-boat operations** (Main Attraction, SeaSquared): These have functional if dated websites and use booking platforms, but their SEO copy is thin, their mobile performance is mediocre, and they're losing organic traffic to TripAdvisor and FishingBooker. They're harder sells — they're already making money — but they're leaving significant direct-booking revenue on the table.

**Tier B — Independent owner-operators** (one captain, one or two boats): This is MCG's highest-value segment. These captains are almost always excellent at fishing and terrible at marketing. Most rely on phone/email only, have outdated WordPress or Wix sites built once and never touched, and have zero SEO presence for the keywords their ideal customers are actually searching. The pitch writes itself: your fishing is world-class, your website is costing you 5–8 trips a month.

The 10 prospects below skew toward independent owner-operators in Key Largo, Tavernier, and Key West — areas not yet covered by existing MCG outreach.

## Tier Legend

- ⚡ **HOT** — Multiple critical failures (no booking, broken/no mobile, no reviews on site, outdated design). High pain, easy pitch, likely revenue-ready decision maker.
- 🔥 **WARM** — 1–2 significant gaps. Good fishing reputation. Website functional but conversion-killing weakness(es) present.
- 🧊 **COLD** — Partial digital presence. Either well-served already or behind a marina/aggregator that makes direct outreach harder.

| **Social Proof** | TripAdvisor linked off-site only; nothing embedded on-site |
| **Last Updated** | January 2026 (per page footer timestamp — minimal update) |

**Why They're HOT:**

Captain Lain is an experienced backcountry and flats guide with a Testimonials page, a Fishing Calendar, and a Fishing Report — he's clearly put thought into the site at some point. But the fundamental conversion problem is there: **no online booking system**. Visitors must call (305) 304-2212 or email to book. In the Florida Keys tourism market, 60–70% of tourists prefer to book online (Phocuswright data). Every tourist browsing at 10pm from their hotel in Key Largo who can't book is gone.

Additionally: the static testimonials page is a missed opportunity. Captain Lain clearly has happy clients. But static text written by the captain ("great time with great clients!") doesn't convert — *live Google or TripAdvisor reviews* do. The gap between "testimonials page exists" and "live social proof widget" is exactly where trust breaks down.

The WordPress theme is heavy on mobile — likely 3–5 second load times on a 4G connection. With 68%+ of Keys tourists browsing on mobile, every second of load time is a bounce.

**Specific Website Weaknesses:**
1. No online booking — call/email only
2. WordPress theme loads slow on mobile (estimated 3–5s on 4G)
3. Static testimonials instead of live review widget
4. No LocalBusiness or TouristTrip structured data
5. Fishing Report section is updated but navigation is clunky — users can't find it easily

**Pitch Angle:** "You've done the hard work — experience, testimonials page, fishing reports. Your website is almost there. The missing piece is a booking system that captures the tourists who find you at 10pm from their phone and can't call. Let me show you how that one fix turns your existing traffic into revenue."

**Estimated Monthly Revenue Loss:** $1,800–$2,750 (primarily from no online booking + mobile load issues)

**Priority:** ⚡ **HOT** — Contact via email first with a personalized audit showing load time and booking gap.

More critically: **no online booking**. Captain Dave explicitly says to "contact Captain Dave at (305) 298-6932 or captaindaveperkins@gmail.com." That's fine for referrals, but it's 2026. If Dave's site doesn't have one, he loses every tourist who can't or won't make a phone call.

The personal bio is strong (teaching background, fishing-camp experience) but it's buried in text. A well-designed site would put his credentials front-and-center with professional photos, a captain bio section, and live review widgets.

1. No online booking — call/email only
2. Reviews and star ratings exist but are off-site only (no embed)
3. 45 years of experience not visually emphasized on the homepage
4. No structured data — Google can't identify this as a tourist activity
5. Hotel pickup differentiator not prominently featured (it's a unique selling point!)

**Pitch Angle:** "Captain Dave, you have 45 years of experience and great reviews — tourists are looking for exactly what you offer. But your website is sending them away to book it from someone else. Let me show you what a booking system and a live review widget would do for your slow months."

**Estimated Monthly Revenue Loss:** $1,650–$2,500

**Priority:** ⚡ **HOT** — Strong human story, huge differentiators, no booking system. Perfect fit.
