# Art Gallery & Studio Prospects — Florida Keys
**Research Date:** 2026-03-22
**Source:** Florida Keys Council of the Arts (keysarts.com), direct site audits, Marathon Florida directory, Islamorada Chamber
**Segment:** Art Galleries & Visual Arts Studios
**Fit:** MCG Consulting web builds $3,500–7,500 + $500/mo maintenance

---

## Research Summary

The Florida Keys Council of the Arts (keysarts.com/arts_directory/galleries-visual-arts/) maintains the definitive directory of galleries and visual arts organizations across the Keys. Of the ~30+ listings, a significant number either link to Facebook as their primary "website," have no website at all, or have functional URLs that resolve to near-empty placeholder pages. These are prime MCG prospects: established local businesses with real foot traffic, real inventory, and real customers — but zero discoverable web presence.

**Key insight:** The gallery segment is clustered in two hotspots: Key West (Duval St corridor) and Islamorada (Morada Way Arts District, MM 81–82). Both are tourism-heavy areas where Google Maps and search visibility directly drives walk-in sales.

## Outreach Approach Notes

**Gallery pitch angle:** "Your art is stunning. Your Google presence isn't."
- Galleries lose customers who find competitors that appear in search first
- Key West and Islamorada gallery districts are competitive — visibility matters
- A website also serves as an always-on sales tool for tourists who visited but didn't buy in person
- Low-ticket easy conversation: $3,500 starting price is often less than a single painting sale

**Referral chain:** Pitch to co-ops (Guild Hall, Artists in Paradise) — every member-artist is a future referral. Pitch to Morada Way — they introduce you to the entire Islamorada gallery district.
