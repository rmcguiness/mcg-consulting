# Spa & Massage Therapy Prospects — Florida Keys
**Research Date:** 2026-03-24
**Source:** TripAdvisor, Yelp, visitfloridakeys.com, marathonflorida.com, oceansir.com, direct site audits
**Segment:** Day Spas, Massage Therapy & Wellness Studios
**Fit:** MCG Consulting web builds $3,500–7,500 + $500/mo maintenance

---

## Research Summary

The Florida Keys spa market splits into two groups that require entirely different sales motions: (1) Resort-attached spas (Cheeca Lodge, Hawk's Cay, Isla Bella Beach Resort, Little Palm Island) with marketing departments and brand standards — not MCG targets — and (2) independent owner-operators who live entirely on word-of-mouth, repeat clients, and the occasional lucky TripAdvisor discovery.

The independent tier is the sweet spot. Most of these operators are deeply skilled at what they do, have earned genuine 5-star reputations, and are leaving significant money on the table because tourists — the highest-value client segment — can't find them. A tourist couple arriving in Marathon for the weekend does not scroll Facebook to find a massage. They Google "couples massage Marathon FL" or "day spa Florida Keys" from their rental house. If a business isn't indexed and searchable with clear pricing and a booking link, that couple books with whoever is.

Several businesses in this tier have no website at all (relying on TripAdvisor/Yelp), operate on free Wix subdomains, or have purely phone-based booking — all symptoms of the same problem: a great local reputation with no digital infrastructure to monetize it beyond word-of-mouth.
