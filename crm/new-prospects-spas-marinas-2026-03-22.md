# Spa & Marina Prospects — Florida Keys
**Research Date:** 2026-03-22
**Source:** visitfloridakeys.com, marathonflorida.com, TripAdvisor, Yelp, marineweb.com, direct site audits
**Segments:** Spas & Massage Therapy | Marina Operators
**Fit:** MCG Consulting web builds $3,500–7,500 + $500/mo maintenance

---

## Research Summary

**Spas & Massage Therapy:** The Florida Keys spa market splits into (1) resort-attached spas with marketing teams (Cheeca Lodge, Hawks Cay, Ocean Key Resort — not targets) and (2) independent operators who run one or two-person operations that live entirely on repeat clients and word-of-mouth referrals. The independent tier is the MCG sweet spot. They have solid reputations and loyal local regulars but virtually no digital discoverability for the tourist market — the highest-value segment. A tourist arriving for a long weekend from Miami does not scroll Facebook. They Google "massage Islamorada" or "spa Key Largo" on their phone while sitting in traffic on US-1. If an operator isn't showing up there, they're invisible.

**Marina Operators:** The Florida Keys has 55+ documented marinas, but the majority of small/mid-size operations rely on phone calls, word-of-mouth, and basic listing pages to manage slip availability and transient dockage. Almost none offer real-time slip availability calendars, online reservation systems, or transparent fuel pricing online. In a tourist market with thousands of visiting boaters each season, this is leaving real money on the dock. The pitch angle is simple: a boater from Fort Lauderdale planning a Keys cruise will call ahead once, maybe twice. If nobody answers, they book somewhere else. A slip availability calendar and online reservation system converts that phone-tag problem into guaranteed revenue.
