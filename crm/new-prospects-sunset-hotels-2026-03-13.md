## Cross-Segment Patterns

1. **No-booking is epidemic** — 5 of 10 prospects have no functional online booking or redirect users off-site. This is the #1 pain point across both segments.
2. **Commission leakage** — Conch City paying 20%+ to Viator/Groupon; lodging prospects have no OTA strategy at all (neither direct nor distributed).
3. **Social media gaps** — Multiple prospects have minimal or zero social presence. Instagram is mandatory for tourism/hospitality in 2026.
4. **Design debt** — Every prospect is running 2015–2020 era themes. None have a truly modern, conversion-optimized site.
5. **Niche stories untold** — Deer Run (vegan/eco), Key Largo Inn (1952 landmark), Ragged Edge (30-year family), Morning Star (cottage+sail) all have compelling brand stories their sites fail to leverage.
