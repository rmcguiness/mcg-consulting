## Research Summary

Round 1 focused heavily on **independent planners and coordinators**. This batch adds (1) underperforming **physical venues** that host weddings and need better conversion-focused websites, and (2) a handful of **planners not captured in Round 1** who have either no website or placeholder-level sites. Physical venues are the highest-revenue pitch because a bride books $5,000–$25,000 in a single transaction from the venue website — bad UX directly kills revenue.

**Key pattern:** Many Keys venues host weddings as a secondary revenue stream (inn, resort, garden club) and have a single text page describing the event space with no photos, no packages, and no inquiry form. These are premium conversion opportunities.

## Outreach Strategy Notes

**Planner vs. venue pitch:**
- **Planners** (I Do Forever, Key Destination, Southernmost, WCO, Soiree): Lead with "bookings you're missing from couples who Google before they call" — ROI is easy math: 1 extra booking/month × $2,500 avg = site paid for in month one
- **Physical venues** (Coconut Palm Inn, Key West Garden Club): Lead with the visual gap — "you're competing with Dolphin Point Villas and Baker's Cay but your site looks like a hotel brochure from 2010"

**Destination wedding timing:** Couples planning Keys weddings are doing so 12–18 months out. Reach out in spring (now) — that's when fall/winter 2026 couples are in research mode.

**SEO angle:** "Florida Keys wedding planner," "Key Largo wedding venue," and "Islamorada wedding" are competitive but very high-intent keywords. A basic on-page SEO structure alone could put a well-built site in the top 5 results within 6 months.

**Upsell path:** Planner builds → venue introductions. A planner who trusts MCG naturally refers their venue partners. Build one relationship, unlock a referral chain.
