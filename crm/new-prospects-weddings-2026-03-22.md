# Wedding & Event Venue Prospects — Florida Keys
**Research Date:** 2026-03-22
**Source:** WeddingWire, The Knot, visitfloridakeys.com, direct site audits, Facebook
**Segment:** Wedding Planners, Event Coordinators & Independent Venue Operators
**Fit:** MCG Consulting web builds $3,500–7,500 + $500/mo maintenance

---

## Research Summary

The Florida Keys wedding industry is large and well-established but heavily skewed toward two types of players: (1) resort hotels with full marketing teams (Cheeca Lodge, Hawks Cay, Baker's Cay, Islander — not targets), and (2) **independent planners and coordinators** who operate as sole proprietors or small teams with thin digital presence.

The independent planner segment is the MCG sweet spot: they have real revenue ($500–$5,000/wedding planning fees × 20–50 weddings/year), established reputations and reviews, but no professional website — just WeddingWire/TheKnot profiles and Facebook pages. A professional website dramatically improves their booking conversion, allows them to rank for "Florida Keys wedding planner" searches, and lets them showcase their portfolio without depending on third-party platforms.

**Key insight:** Many of these planners have 100+ reviews on WeddingWire/TheKnot but **zero standalone web presence.** They're already booked through word-of-mouth, which means a website would be **incremental** revenue — pure upside with no risk of disrupting existing bookings. This is an easy sell.

## Outreach Approach Notes

**Wedding planner pitch angle:** "Your reputation is on WeddingWire. Your revenue depends on what couples find when they Google you instead."
- Most Keys planners book 20–60 weddings per year at $1,500–$5,000 avg planning fee
- Even capturing 3–5 additional bookings per year from organic search easily justifies a $5,000 website
- Destination wedding couples plan 12–18 months out and research extensively online
- Instagram/Pinterest-driven couples expect a portfolio — a professional website is now table stakes

**Seasonal pitch:** Florida Keys wedding season peaks October–May. Reach out now (March) — summer is when planners have bandwidth to work on their own business.

**Referral angle:** Planners know photographers, florists, caterers, DJs, officiants — all potential MCG clients. One planner relationship = access to a vendor network of 10–20 other small businesses.
