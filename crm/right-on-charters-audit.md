## The 3 Issues Costing You Money

### 1. Your "Book Now" Button Is Broken — Every Click Is a Lost Booking

This is the single most expensive problem on your website. Your "Book Now" button is non-functional — it doesn't connect to any booking system, calendar, or even a contact form. It's a dead end.

Here's what that means in real numbers:

- A Marathon charter site in your position gets ~350–550 unique visitors/month
- Roughly 15–20% of those visitors (50–110 people) are actively looking to book
- Your "Book Now" button is the #1 thing they click — and nothing happens
- Industry data shows **72% of customers who hit a broken booking flow will leave and book a competitor** — they don't come back
- That's **36–79 potential customers per month** who wanted to give you money and couldn't

Your competitors at Captain Hooks Marina with working FareHarbor integrations are catching these customers. Every day that button stays broken is money left on the dock.

**Estimated monthly loss: $1,650–$2,750**

### 2. No Reviews or Testimonials — Visitors Have No Reason to Trust You

Your site has zero customer reviews, zero testimonials, and zero social proof of any kind. For a tourist choosing between 15+ Marathon fishing charters, this is a dealbreaker.

Here's how fishing charter customers actually decide:

- **92% of tourists** read online reviews before booking an activity (BrightLocal 2025)
- The average customer reads **6–10 reviews** before feeling confident enough to book
- Charters with visible reviews on their website convert at **2.3x the rate** of those without

If you have Google reviews, TripAdvisor ratings, or happy repeat customers — none of that credibility is visible on your website. A tourist comparing you to a competitor who has a 4.8-star rating displayed prominently will choose the competitor every time, even if your fishing is better.

A simple live Google review widget on your homepage would immediately change this dynamic.

**Estimated monthly loss: $550–$825**

### 3. Mixed Photo Quality Undermines Your Credibility

Your gallery has some great shots — real catches, happy customers, the Marathon backcountry. But they're mixed in with low-resolution, poorly lit, or blurry photos that drag down the overall impression.

Here's why this matters:

- **Photos are the #1 factor** in charter selection after price and reviews (Phocuswright)
- Low-res images signal "unprofessional" — even if the fishing is world-class
- Inconsistent photo quality creates a "some good, some bad" impression that erodes trust
- Mobile users (68% of your traffic) see these quality issues even more clearly on retina screens

Your best photos are genuinely good. The problem is they're competing with shots that don't belong on a professional charter website. A curated gallery with 20 great photos beats 60 mixed-quality photos every time.

**Estimated monthly loss: $275–$550**

## Bonus Issue: Keyword-Stuffed SEO Is Hurting You

Your page copy reads like it was written for Google, not for customers. Phrases like "Marathon fishing charter fishing trips Marathon FL charter fishing" stuffed into content blocks don't help your rankings anymore — Google's algorithms have penalized keyword stuffing since 2015. Worse, it makes your site feel spammy and inauthentic to real visitors.

Modern SEO is about natural, helpful content that answers the questions your customers are actually asking: "What kind of fishing can I do in Marathon?" "Is fly fishing good in the Keys in March?" "How much does a half-day charter cost?"

## What We'd Build for Right On Charters

Your Squarespace foundation isn't terrible — but we'd rebuild on a platform that gives you more control and better performance:

1. **Working booking system with FareHarbor** — real-time availability calendar connected to a "Book Now" button that actually works. Customers pick their trip type (fly fishing, light tackle, spin fishing), select a date, choose half-day or full-day, and pay a deposit. This is the #1 priority and the single biggest ROI item.

2. **Live Google review widget** — your best reviews pulled automatically onto the homepage and trip pages. 5-star ratings visible the moment someone lands on your site. Updated in real time as new reviews come in.

3. **Professional photo gallery** — curated selection of your best catches and customer moments. WebP format, lazy-loaded, organized by trip type and species. Every photo high-res, well-lit, and worth showing. We'll help you identify which shots to feature and which to cut.

4. **Clean, natural SEO copy** — rewritten page content that targets the right keywords ("Marathon fly fishing charter," "Keys backcountry fishing guide") naturally and conversationally. Proper title tags, meta descriptions, and LocalBusiness schema markup.

5. **Mobile-optimized design** — clean layout that loads fast on any device. Large tap targets, easy navigation, prominent booking button on every page. Built for the tourist browsing on their phone at the hotel.

6. **Trip detail pages** — dedicated pages for each trip type with real information: what you'll target, what's included, seasonal notes, and transparent pricing. No filler, no keyword stuffing — just honest content that helps customers choose.
